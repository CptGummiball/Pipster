# Pipster Wiki

Pipster adds readable pipes for **items, fluids, gas and energy** to Minecraft, with five tiers,
filters right at the port and channel-based tesseracts. It runs on **Fabric** and **NeoForge**.

> Pipster is in **pre-release**. Balance values and save data can still change before 1.0.

## The short version

1. Craft a few **Copper Item Pipes** and a **Conduit Wrench**.
2. Connect two chests with the pipes. Pipes connect to each other and to anything with an
   inventory on their own.
3. Right-click the pipe end at the source chest with the wrench and set the port to **Extract**.
4. Items now move from the source chest to the other chest.
